Insights and advocacy

Examining evidence, identifying systemic gaps, and championing equitable solutions for paid and unpaid caregivers across the care continuum.

Safe hospital-to-home transitions and shared accountability

Unsafe hospital discharges frequently leave family caregivers unprepared for complex post-acute clinical responsibilities. The 7th Standard™ advocates for structured institutional accountability through environmental readiness and safety audits, ensuring care plans genuinely match home conditions, required equipment, and clear instructions rather than shifting blame onto families.

Credentialing pathways and workforce equity

Direct care workers face fragmented regulations, inconsistent competency standards, and inequitable compensation. Through accessible Atomic Learning Elements (ALEs) and caregiver-owned stackable credentials, we provide verified, portable education that validates real-world skill and paves the way for higher industry recognition, fair wages, and career longevity.

Burnout prevention and practical caregiving support

Preventable harm and severe caregiver burnout thrive in isolation. We share actionable policy briefs, pilot findings from Indiana and Washington, and ethical support technology—including the Caregiver Confidence Console—to safeguard mental health, facilitate early intervention, and promote sustainable care ecosystems nationwide.
