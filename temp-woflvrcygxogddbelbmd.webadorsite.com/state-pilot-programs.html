State pilot programs in Indiana and Washington

The 7th Standard™ is preparing initial pilot programs designed to evaluate our caregiver education, environmental readiness audits, and stackable credentialing model in real-world settings. Join us in shaping an evidence-based standard of care.

Shaping care in diverse environments

Our planned pilots explore identical core principles—safety, demonstrated competency, equity, and human-governed technology—adapted across distinct community and regulatory landscapes. Confidence is the 7th Standard™.

Indiana pilot focus: Community and family support

In Indiana, the pilot emphasizes community-based caregiver needs. We focus on caregiver preparedness, health literacy, resource accessibility, and critical support for both unpaid family caregivers and non-medical frontline staff. By testing our Caregiver Confidence Console™ and environmental audits, we establish measurable baselines for home safety and caregiver well-being.

Washington pilot focus: Regulatory integration and workforce portability

In Washington, our evaluation examines how the foundational model thrives within a comprehensive regulatory and workforce structure. We assess credential portability, institutional adoption, and organizational safety workflows. Note that all pilots remain in planning, and formal participation criteria are finalized in partnership with regional stakeholders.
