Core pillars of our ecosystem

We bridge the divide between acute care facilities and home environments through human-governed technology, practical training, and continuous caregiver support.

Personalized training

Culturally responsive, multilingual learning pathways offering stackable credentials owned by the caregiver ensuring full equity for everyone no matter where they are in their caregiver journey and allowing anyone to meet where they are for better results and true confidence in their role.

View solutions

Caregiver confidence console

A dedicated tool for documenting baseline, maintaining accurate time in place, logging scope changes, tracking caregiver self-care, receiving journal prompts, addressing concerns, finding dedicated resources like respite, continued education. and more.

Learn more

Safety and readiness audit

To ensure safe transitions to home for recovery or long term care,  discharging organizations must become accountable  by environmental readiness and safety audits matching care plans with real conditions which in turn  promotes  client and caregiver confidence.

Our driving principles

The 7th Standard™ is dedicated to elevating the caregiving journey through systemic transformation, human governance, and comprehensive support. Our work is anchored in three vital commitments.

Educate

Advocate

Empower

Delivering multilingual, culturally responsive training pathways and stackable credentials that caregivers own and carry with pride across every stage of their career.

Holding discharging facilities accountable for safe transitions home, ending caregiver blame, and pushing for policy shifts that secure fair wages and systemic recognition.

Providing practical tools like the Caregiver Confidence Console to monitor baselines, log scope changes, safeguard well-being, and prevent avoidable readmissions.

Our Mission:

Educate   Advocate   Empower

The 7th Standard™ is dedicated to transforming caregiving from an overlooked obligation into a recognized, supported profession. We integrate modern AI capability with indispensable human governance to provide actionable resources, protect caregiver well-being, and protect patients during vulnerable transition phases.
