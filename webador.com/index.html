Frequently asked questions

Can I really do this myself, with no technical skills?

Yes, and that's exactly who we build for. There's no code anywhere: you click on something and change it. And when you do get stuck, a real person from our support team will help you out.

Can I change anything the AI makes?

Every word, photo, color, and page. You can even switch to a completely different design without losing your content, or start again from scratch. AI writes the first draft; you always have the final say.

Will my website look professional, even though I'm no designer?

Yes, and that part isn't on you. Every design is made by professionals, and your text, photos, and colors fall into place automatically. You can't really break it: whatever you change stays tidy and works on every screen.

How soon can my website be online?

Your first version is ready about a minute after you describe your business. Most people then spend an afternoon making it truly theirs, adding their own photos and their own words, before they publish. Nothing is visible to the public until you decide to go live.

Will customers be able to find me on Google?

Your website is made with everything Google looks for already in place, so there's nothing for you to set up. Do give it a little time, however: new websites need a few weeks to settle into search results, and your own domain name helps you gets there sooner.

Can I get an email address linked to my own domain name?

Yes. An email address like info@yourbusiness.com is just a few clicks away with our Pro and Business plans. Pro comes with a 1GB mailbox, Business with an XL mailbox that offers 10GB of storage space. Customers tend to trust an address linked to your own domain name more than a personal one.

What's included in my plan?

Everything your website needs to run: hosting, your design, AI features, updates, and our support. There are no plug-ins to buy and nothing extra to install. Your domain name is free for the first year. See our pricing page for current subscription pricing.

Can I try it before I pay anything?

Yes. Build your whole website, take as long as you like, and see exactly how it looks. You only start paying when you're ready to publish it on your own domain name. There's no countdown clock.

Is my website really mine?

Yes. Your domain name is registered in your name, not ours, and everything you write and upload stays yours. If you ever move on, we'll help you obtain your domain transfer code, with no hoops to jump through.

What if my question isn't answered here?

Ask us! Our support team answers real questions from real beginners every day, and our help center is full of short guides and videos. You won't be figuring this out alone.
