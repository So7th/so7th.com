Frequently asked questions

Can I use my own domain name and email address?

Yes, you can register a new domain name, but you can also use a domain name you already own. You can transfer the domain to us or link it to our server via your provider.

Will I be the owner of my domain name?

Yes, we'll register the domain name under your name. This means you can transfer the domain name to another provider at any time.

Can I change my plan later?

Yes, you can upgrade at any time without losing any of your website's content. You can also return to a cheaper plan, but this will result in the loss of some features.

What is the duration of the contract?

If you opt for monthly payment, your subscription can be canceled at any time with a notice period of 1 month. If you pay annually, your subscription will be automatically renewed for a year, unless you've canceled at least 1 month before the renewal date.

How can I pay for my subscription?

You can easily pay the monthly or annual costs for your subscription via credit card or PayPal.

Do you provide support?

We offer support for beginners as well as our more technically-inclined customers. Having trouble with something? We're happy to help!

Is web hosting included with my website?

Yes, web hosting is included in all our plans. Webador really is a one-stop shop!

Are there any hidden restrictions?

No. You can add unlimited pages and upload unlimited images with any Webador plan (in accordance with the Fair Use Policy). The mailboxes included in every plan come with a whopping 1GB capacity, so you don't have to worry about storage space or data usage!

Can I order additional domain names or mailboxes?

You can order additional domain names ($22.50 per year) and mailboxes ($17.50 per year) at any time.
