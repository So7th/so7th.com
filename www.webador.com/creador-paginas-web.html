Frequently asked questions

Is the free website maker really 100% free?

Webador's free version is completely free to use, for as long as you like. If you'd like access to professional features, you can choose from one of our paid subscriptions. A Webador Pro plan costs just $14.00 (excl. VAT) per month. In return, you get your own domain name, online store features, and a completely ad-free website.

How much storage space and bandwidth do I get with a Webador website?

All our plans offer unlimited storage space and data traffic based on fair use. Please contact us if you have any questions about this. Uploading HD videos to our servers is only possible with a Business plan. You can, however, embed (your own) YouTube videos on your Webador website at any time.

Do I need to understand code or learn programming first?

No. Our website builder is designed in such a way that you don't need any technical experience. We don't use tricky computer jargon, and all coding is done automatically for you by Webador. With our user-friendly editor, you can create your own website quickly and easily.

What does it cost to use our website builder?

Webador offers a free plan, so you can build a website at no cost. If you'd like additional features such as a custom domain name and the option to list products for sale, we offer a Pro subscription for just $126.00 (excl. VAT) per year. Webador is one of the cheapest website builders in the world, and using our service means you don't have to spend hundreds (if not thousands) on hiring a web developer.

Can I use my own domain name for my site?

Yes, you can use a domain name to link to your website. Do you already have a domain name? If so, you can transfer it to us or link it to our server via your provider. Or you can register a new domain name with us at any time. You can also easily create professional email addresses with your domain.

Can I add an online store to my website?

You can start selling products with a Pro or Business plan. With our Editor, putting your own products on your site is quick and easy. You can also arrange payment processing via Stripe in order to safely accept online payments.

How long does it take to master Webador's editor?

The Webador website builder is designed in such a way that even complete beginners will quickly get to grips with how it works. Almost everything can be reached in a few clicks. We offer free (video) tutorials and an extensive help center. If you're still struggling to find a solution, you can always contact our experienced customer support team.

Do we offer customer support?

Need help creating your website? Having technical issues? Don't panic! Our experienced support team is here to help, and we'll have you back to work in no time.
