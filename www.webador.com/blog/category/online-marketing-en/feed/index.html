]]> Black Friday falls on Friday, November 27 this year. Many startup entrepreneurs think that Black Friday is only for the ‘big market leaders’. But did you know that on Black Friday 2025, shoppers worldwide spent around $79 billion online, 6% more than the year before? In the US alone, online spending hit a record $11.8 billion, up from $10.8 billion in 2024 (TechCrunch). So it’s only increasing, and it’s not just the big retailers who benefit: smaller webshops get their share too!

Something has changed compared to a few years ago, however: Black Friday is no longer a one-day event. Over Cyber Week 2025, the five days from Thanksgiving to Cyber Monday, US shoppers spent $44.2 billion online (Adobe). Earlier promotions also kept spending steady throughout November and December instead of concentrating it in a single week. In other words, people are buying earlier and comparing prices for longer.

So, offering the highest discount isn’t necessary to stand out. In this blog, you’ll read 7 practical tips to approach Black Friday 2026 smartly.

#1: Start on time and determine your goal

Because customers are shopping earlier and earlier, waiting until the last week is a waste. Shoppers are starting earlier than ever: more than half of Americans begin their holiday shopping by October. Many of them hold off on bigger purchases until the Black Friday deals appear

Therefore, first ask yourself what you want to achieve with your offer. After all, many consumers discover a new online store for the first time during Black Friday. As a startup, you need to capitalize on that!
Also, take a look back: which products sold well this year, and which ones didn’t sell well? Those figures will help you come up with a smart promotion.

#2: Choose an action that suits you

Black Friday isn’t just about mega discounts. As a small business owner, you probably can’t win this price war anyway, and you don’t have to. Instead, choose a promotion that suits your brand:

  • Bundles : combine 2 or 3 products into an affordable set that you offer only during Black Friday.
  • Free shipping : for many customers an extra push to order
  • A little extra : such as a gift with orders from a certain amount
  • Discount on a selection : choose a top 5 of the most popular products and offer a competitive deal on them.

Don’t forget to mention your service on your website as well! Smaller providers often offer better and more personal service, and they help customers with problems faster. So be sure to put that in the spotlight on your website!

#3: Give a real discount (and follow the rules)

Around Black Friday, consumer watchdogs pay extra attention to fake discounts. The exact rules depend on where you sell, but the principle is the same everywhere: the “was” price you compare against must be a real price that you actually charged recently. In the EU, including Ireland, it’s spelled out precisely: your discount must be based on the lowest price you charged in the 30 days before the sale. Raising your prices just before Black Friday to make the discount look bigger is therefore a no-go.

For you as a starting business, this is actually good news. Fair prices build trust, and trust is exactly what customers look for in a new webshop. So set your prices right now, and you can run your Black Friday promotion with peace of mind.

#4: Prepare your webshop for the rush

During Cyber Week 2025, 70% of all online orders worldwide were placed on a mobile device, and 78% of online traffic came from phones and tablets (Chain Store Age, citing Salesforce). In other words, most of your Black Friday customers will see your webshop on a small screen.

Your webshop must therefore work just as well on mobile as on your laptop. So, review the following points:

  • Open your webshop on your phone and place a test order. Do all the buttons work and is the text easy to read?
  • Reduce the size of large photos before uploading them. Heavy images cause your website to load more slowly.
  • Ensure your payment methods work. Make sure your payments such as Stripe and PayPal are working correctly.
  • Create a separate Black Friday promotions page.

#5: Ensure good planning

The best promotions yield nothing if no one knows about them. You stand out with a clear plan and a personal touch. An example of a plan for Black Friday 2026:

  • Early November: have visitors/customers subscribe to your newsletter so they see your Black Friday deal first.
  • Week before Black Friday: give a sneak peek of your Black Friday promotion on your social media channels.
  • Thursday, November 26 – the day before Black Friday: reward your loyal customers with ‘early access’ or an extra discount code.
  • Friday, November 27 – Black Friday : your Black Friday deal is live! Share this on all your channels!
  • Monday, November 30 – Cyber ​​Monday: Is your promotion running until Cyber ​​Monday? If so, please let us know and send a final reminder.

#6: Manage your shipping, inventory, and returns

Parcel deliverers work overtime around Black Friday. During Black Friday week 2025, DHL broke its parcel record in Germany: on a single day, its network sorted around 12.4 million shipments, almost twice as many as on a normal day (DHL). Carriers everywhere are under pressure in this period, so plan your shipping well in advance. Here is how to avoid disappointed customers:

  • Check your stock: Make sure you have enough stock of your promotional products.
  • Be honest about your delivery times: state the average delivery time in days on your website.
  • Put your return policy on your website: customers have a 14-day cooling-off period for online purchases, even for discounted products. Place your return policy in the footer of your website so that it is always easy to find.

#7: Turn new buyers into regular customers

How you approach customers after their purchase determines whether they return to you ( DHL ). So Black Friday is not the end, but rather the beginning.

  • A personal thank you included with the package or in an email sticks with you.
  • Ask for a review from new customers.
  • After Cyber ​​Monday, Sinterklaas and Christmas are just around the corner. Let your customers know that you are already working on it!

Ready for your first Black Friday?

With these tips, you are fully prepared! A fair promotion, a well-functioning webshop, and a personal approach help you stand out among the big names as a startup. We wish you a successful Black Friday!

1: Create a Special Halloween page 

Do you have products you can give a Halloween makeover? Or do you want to offer a temporary special promotion? Then create a separate Halloween page on your website.
A dedicated page ensures that visitors don’t have to search through your entire webshop. All Halloween-related products, offers, and inspiration are gathered in one place.

You can dress up the entire page in Halloween style: for example, add a matching banner and make products stand out with a special Halloween product label . You don’t have to overhaul your entire branding; a few small details can be enough.

To make the page stand out, you can send visitors from the homepage to the Halloween page. For example, place a prominent button in your banner with the text “View our Halloween Deals” . Link the button directly to your Halloween page. 

2: Give your visitors a reason to order now

A holiday is a good time to organize a temporary promotion. Not because you necessarily have to offer a discount, but because a temporary offer can give visitors just that extra push to make a purchase. 

A simple way to draw attention to a promotion is with the top bar , where you can display a specific discount.
You can also choose to include a small gift with an order, such as a bag of Halloween candy. If you then link a minimum order amount to this, for example starting at 50 euros, you immediately increase the customer’s order value. 

Tip: ensure that the terms and conditions of your promotions are clear. State when the promotion ends, the minimum order value, and whether stock is limited. 

3: Create urgency with a countdown

Halloween is just one day, and you can use that very temporary nature to encourage visitors to make a purchase. A countdown timer on your website shows how much time is left for your promotion. 
You can use an external tool like Elfsight for this . With their countdown widget, you can create a timer and then place it on your website using the Embed code element.

A countdown works especially well when you link it to an actual deadline. So use it for a temporary promotion, a final order date, or a Halloween offer. That way, the timer actually has meaning for your visitor.

4: Give your logo a Halloween twist

Do you want to take the Halloween theme a little further? Then you can also give your logo a small update! 
You don’t have to design a completely new logo for this; even a subtle adjustment can be fun. Think, for example, of a spider web, a pumpkin, or a witch’s hat. 

With AI tools, you can nowadays very easily try out ideas. Upload your existing logo to an AI tool and indicate that you want to create a Halloween version of it. 

For example: ‘Subtly add a small spiderweb and a witch’s hat to my logo. Retain the original colors, style, and recognizability of the logo.’

Have you created a nice version? Then you can replace it in Webador in the places where your logo is used. Think of the website icon and the logo on your homepage . 

Ready to scare your webshop?

As a starting entrepreneur, you probably don’t have time to prepare a Halloween campaign for weeks, but fortunately, you can already make a difference with a few small adjustments!

Use Halloween primarily as an opportunity to take a fresh look at your webshop. Which products deserve some extra attention? Can you come up with a temporary promotion? And how do you give visitors that final push to place an order today?

You don’t have to go big. Pick one or two ideas that suit your webshop, give them a creative Halloween twist, and see if it pays off. This way, you turn Halloween not only into a fun holiday but also into a great opportunity to draw extra attention to your webshop.

For some, it’s the revolution of the year; for others, an exciting new playing field. What does the arrival of TikTok Shop mean for you as a new entrepreneur? And how can you adapt your online store to this trend? In this blog, we’ll walk you through it and explain everything!

The opportunities

The biggest advantage of TikTok Shop is that you don’t have to start from scratch. Your products are immediately shown to an audience that actively uses the platform and is interested in this type of video. The barrier between discovery and purchase has never been lower: see a cool gadget or trendy outfit? Just tap the product tag and it’s already ordered!

The downside

Although the reach is appealing, TikTok Shop also comes with strict regulations and costs:

  1. The commission: Of course, TikTok doesn’t offer this for free. The platform commission in Europe and the UK has been 9%. In America it is around 6%. That’s a significant chunk of your profit margin.
  2. Quick turnaround: TikTok’s algorithm can reward you with thousands of orders today and ignore you tomorrow. Can your logistics handle that sudden spike in orders? TikTok penalizes online stores that deliver late or make administrative errors!

What about Webador and TikTok Shop?

At this time, we don’t have a direct, built-in integration with TikTok Shop. TikTok often requires a platform that uses a public API to synchronize products and inventory in real time for an official live integration.

Fortunately, that doesn’t mean you can’t do anything about it! If you use Webador, there are still two smart ways you can capitalize on the TikTok hype:

Method 1: The Link-in-Bio

Instead of letting users check out directly within the TikTok app, you redirect them to your own online store.

  • Business Account: Switch to a TikTok Business account for free. This allows you to include a clickable link to your website in your bio.
  • Call-to-Action: Create videos about your products and always end with a call-to-action. For example: “Click the link in my bio to order.”

Method 2: Selling manually through TikTok Seller Center

Are you eager to use TikTok Shop? If so, you can manually create a shop through the TikTok Seller Center.

  • Manual entry: You manually enter your current products, images, and prices on the TikTok platform.
  • Keep an eye on your inventory: Since there is no automatic integration, your inventory will not sync. If you sell something on TikTok, you must manually update the inventory in your Webador online store!

Opportunity or Risk?

Is TikTok Shop a goldmine? Or more of an e-commerce pitfall? The answer is probably somewhere in between.

By using TikTok to create content and direct visitors to your own website via your bio, you can expand your reach without having to deal with the platform’s associated costs.

]]> Email campaigns remain a powerful tool for both businesses and content creators. Creating an effective email goes beyond simply hitting “send”, it’s about connecting with your audience. 

In this blog, we’ll take you through some examples to improve your email campaigns and make a lasting impact! 

1. Know your audience

Understanding your audience is the key to a successful email campaign. Take the time to understand their preferences, interests, and behavior. Target your email list based on these factors to send targeted and relevant content. 

You can learn more about your audience by asking them directly about their preferences through surveys, tracking what they click on in your emails, monitoring social media comments, and paying attention to feedback you receive.

2. Write compelling subject lines

The subject line is the first thing recipients see, so make sure it’s valuable. Keep it concise, engaging, and relevant to entice recipients to open the email. An effective subject line might be, for example, “Last chance for 20% off.” 

To avoid your emails being marked as spam, do not use capitalization or excessive punctuation.

3. Focus on quality content

Provide value with your email content. Whether it’s informative articles, exclusive offers, or entertaining stories, ensure your content aligns with your audience’s expectations. To understand these expectations, pay attention to audience feedback through surveys, social media interactions, and comments. Analyze the content that gets the most engagement and adjust your future emails accordingly. 

Tip: Use colloquial language to make your emails more accessible and engaging. 

4. Optimize for mobile

Given the growing number of users checking their email on their phones, it’s crucial to optimize your email campaigns for smaller screens. Make sure your emails are responsive and easy to read across devices.  

5. Clear Call-to-Action

Every email should have a clear objective, and your Call to Action (CTA) should guide your readers to the next step. Whether it’s making a purchase, subscribing, or downloading content, a compelling CTA increases the chance of conversion. 

6. Respect Privacy and Consents

Building trust is crucial in email marketing. If people trust your brand, they’re more likely to open your emails. To earn this trust, always ask for permission to send emails. Make sure you have your subscribers’ explicit consent before sending them emails. Respect their privacy, provide an easy way to unsubscribe, and show that you care about their choices.

7. Personalize your emails

Personalization creates a sense of connection and increases the likelihood that recipients will appreciate your emails. Make your emails special by using people’s names and talking about things they enjoy. Imagine receiving an email that knows your name and suggests things you’re interested in. It feels like the sender really knows you, and you’re more likely to read their email. 

8. Maintain long-term relationships 

Email campaigns aren’t just about making a one-time sale: they’re about building long-term relationships. Implement drip campaigns and automation to nurture leads over time. Offer valuable content at different stages of the customer journey to keep your readers engaged. Think of emailing as making friends, not selling your services. Sending regular updates and helpful information is like staying in touch with a friend. 

Conclusion

Mastering email marketing requires a strategic approach. Email campaigns aren’t just a tool; they’re a powerful tool in your marketing strategy. By following these 8 steps, you can build lasting relationships and increase the success of your website.

]]> Every year, numerous new online shops are launched, but unfortunately, not all of them are successful. This is often due to common mistakes that can easily be avoided with the right knowledge. Whether you are just starting out with your online shop or have been around for a while and want to improve your results, these insights will help you on your way to a successful online business.

In this blog, you will learn about the seven most common online shop mistakes and, of course, we will also tell you how to avoid them!

1. Outdated or incorrect content

This is a classic: online shops with outdated information. Think of promotions that have long since expired, outdated holiday notifications or public holidays that have already passed. This comes across as extremely unprofessional and can scare off potential customers.

Imagine walking into a physical shop where the Christmas decorations are still up in March; that doesn’t make a good impression. Online, this is just as important. Customers expect up-to-date and relevant information.

  • Solution: Make it a habit to regularly check all your pages, not just your home page. Ensure that content is up-to-date and disable temporary notifications as soon as they are no longer relevant.

2. A confusing menu

An overcrowded menu with too many categories can confuse visitors and cause them to get lost on your website. When a customer cannot quickly find what they are looking for, there is a good chance that they will abandon their search and go to a competitor. Think from the customer’s perspective: what are they looking for?

  • Solution: Limit your menu to only the most important pages. Create submenus to go into more detail on certain pages. Have your menu tested by family and friends to see if they can easily find their way around!

3. Lack of high-quality product photos

Online shopping is all about visual presentation; you have to appeal to the eye! Consumers cannot hold products in their hands, feel them, or experience the atmosphere of a physical shop. Good product photos are therefore crucial.

  • Solution: Invest in clear product photos. Provide multiple angles of your product, detailed photos and atmospheric images showing the product in use. Ensure consistency in your images so that your product page forms a coherent whole.

4. No clear niche

When you try to sell everything to everyone, you often end up selling nothing to anyone. The online market is saturated, and you stand out by specializing. A broad focus leads to a lot of competition and makes it difficult to formulate a clear marketing message. A niche allows you to focus on a specific, smaller group with specific needs.

  • Solution: Choose a niche. Focus on a specific target group or product group. This reduces competition and makes your marketing more targeted and effective. It helps you build a loyal customer base. So think about what makes you unique and where your expertise lies.

5. Incorrect VAT handling and administration

VAT handling can be particularly complex in international sales. Errors in this area can lead to problems with the tax authorities. Accurate administration is not only a legal obligation, but also essential for a smoothly running online business.

  • Solution: Ensure that your VAT settings are correct. For business customers abroad, VAT is often reversed (intra-Community supply). Therefore, always check your customer’s VAT number.

6. Missing company information and terms and conditions

Consumers want to know who they are doing business with. Transparency builds trust and is required by law. The absence of essential contact and company information can lead to suspicion among your visitors. Customers want to know that they are buying from a legitimate company that they can contact if they have questions or problems.

  • Solution: Clearly state your company name, company registration number, address, VAT number, telephone number and email address on your website. Preferably on a separate contact page and in your footer. Also be clear about which payment and shipping methods you offer. Don’t forget to draw up your terms and conditions, privacy policy and cookie policy.

7. Forgetting to communicate the return policy

The right of withdrawal is an important right for online buyers and a legal obligation for webshop owners. A clear return policy creates trust and can lower the threshold for purchasing.

  • Solution: Clearly state how long the cooling-off period is (usually 14 days after receipt of the product) and how customers can make use of it. Communicate clearly about the costs of returning the product, the procedure, and the refund period.

]]> A successful webshop is not just about beautiful products: the way you present them is just as important. Good product descriptions are crucial.
A good product description is not just listing the features of your product. It functions as a powerful tool to inform, inspire and convince potential customers of the value of your product. Product descriptions are the key to converting website visitors into loyal customers.

Why a good product description can make all the difference

Many new entrepreneurs underestimate the power of a strong product description. They often see it as an obligation, which of course it is, but in reality it is one of the most influential factors in the customer’s decision-making process.
If you have a good product description, it helps with:

  • Improving your search engine visibility (SEO): By naturally incorporating relevant keywords into your descriptions, you increase the chance that potential customers will find your product via Google and other search engines.
  • Credibility of your business: A well-written, informative and honest description shows that you know what you are talking about. This helps to build long-term customer relationships.
  • More sales: This is of course your goal! A good description removes doubts from the customer, so that they will click ‘add to cart’ faster.

But now the question is, how do you write product descriptions that sell? We have written a step-by-step plan that can help you on your way:

Step 1: Know your ideal customer

Before you start adding products to your webshop, you need to have a good understanding of who is buying your products. Ask yourself the following questions:

  • Who is my ideal customer? Think of age, gender, location but also think of interests and values.
  • What problem does my product solve for them? People buy something for a solution to their problem or a fulfillment of their need. What is the ‘pain’ that your product takes away?
  • What type of language do they use? For example, is your target audience formal or informal?

Example: an online store that makes handmade, sustainable children’s clothing.
Correct: “Our clothing is made from organic cotton, which is soft on your child’s sensitive skin and lasts a long time, even after countless washes.”
Incorrect: “Our clothing is made from 100% cotton.”

Step 2: Focus on benefits of your products

This is perhaps the most important thing about writing a good product description. Customers may not be interested in what a product is, but rather in what it does for them. Every function of your product should be translated into a direct benefit for the customer.

Example: a webshop that offers computers.
Correct : “Experience lightning-fast performance with this laptop, even with the most demanding software. Thanks to the powerful 16GB RAM and Intel Core 17 processor, you will no longer experience long waiting times when opening a browser.”
Incorrect : “This laptop has 16GB RAM and an Intel Core 17 processor.”

Do you see the difference? The first example explains why those specifications are important to the customer and how it can improve their daily life or work. While the second example only describes what features the laptop has, so the customer still doesn’t know what they can do with it or what the benefits are.

Step 3: Use persuasive language

Your product description should be vivid and engaging. Use words that evoke an emotional response and help the reader imagine themselves using the product.

  • Emotional language: Words like “ effortless ,” “ luxurious ,” “ pampering ,” “ ultimate ,” “ exclusive ,” or “ transform ” can evoke desire.
  • Sensory language: Describe how something feels, smells, tastes, sounds, or looks.
  • Metaphors and similes:
    – “This product is a lifesaver for busy mornings.”
    – “Our app is like a personal assistant for your finances.”

This gives the customer an understanding of what the slippers will feel like after a long day.

Step 4: Make it easy to read

Online shoppers have short attention spans. They scan pages looking for relevant information. Make sure your descriptions are easy to read and digest. Use the following tips:

  • Short paragraphs: Break long texts into short pieces, a maximum of 3-4 sentences per paragraph is a good guideline.
  • Use bullet points: Ideal for listing benefits or features.
  • Bold text: Highlight the most important words in the sentence. Don’t do this with too many words!
  • White space: Provide sufficient white space around your text, this makes it pleasant to read.

Conclusion: Write catchy product descriptions and generate more sales

Writing compelling product descriptions is an art, but it’s one that can easily be learned and perfected. It’s not just about listing what you’re selling, it’s about creating an experience and solving problems.

Start applying these strategies to your own product pages today. Analyze your current descriptions, rewrite them with your ideal customer and benefits in mind, and monitor the results. You’ll see that the time and effort you put into this will pay off in increased sales and happier customers!

]]> What is Keyword research?

Keyword research involves identifying the exact words and phrases people use when searching online for information, products, or services on search engines like Google.

For instance, if you own a local café, understanding common searches such as “best coffee near me” or “fresh pastries in [your city]” is crucial. This knowledge allows you to tailor your online content to match these specific queries.

Whether you are a business, blogger, or marketer, effective keyword research improves your website’s visibility in search engine results, leading to increased traffic, engagement, and potential customer conversions.

Why is Keyword Research important?

Keyword research is an essential task for website owners trying to grow their online visibility and understand customer search behavior.

Although it may not be the most engaging activity, consistent keyword research maximizes the potential to connect with the right audience. Updating and using the correct keywords helps your website or blog gets found before your competitors.

Here are five key benefits of prioritizing keyword research:

  • Boost SEO and improve rankings: Strategic keywords help search engines better understand and index your content, leading to higher placement in search results.
  • Generate organic traffic: By targeting relevant keywords, you attract users who are actively searching for information, products, or services you offer, resulting in potential customer loyalty.
  • Gain insights into audience intent: Keyword research reveals the language and queries your target audience uses, providing valuable understanding of their needs.
  • Establish a competitive edge: Identifying and effectively using relevant keywords allows you to outperform competitors in search engine results pages and capture a larger share of the target market.
  • Achieve higher conversion rates: By attracting visitors who are genuinely interested in what you provide, keyword research contributes to a greater likelihood of conversions, whether it’s sales, leads, or other desired actions.

How to get started with Keyword Research:

To effectively conduct keyword research for your content, SEO, or marketing strategy, follow these essential steps:

  1. Define Your Objectives: Clearly identify what you aim to achieve. Do you want to increase website traffic or drive more sales? Aligning your keywords with specific goals is fundamental.
  2. Brainstorm Initial Keywords: Begin by thinking broadly about your product or service. What are the core topics? Examples include “coffee,” “stationary,” or “digital downloads.” Generate as many simple, foundational keywords as possible and then refine this list to align with your offerings and objectives.
  3. Leverage Keyword Research Tools: Utilize available tools like Google Keyword Planner, Ubersuggest, and SEMrush. These platforms can provide valuable insights into relevant keywords for your brand and product/service, often highlighting top-performing options.
  4. Identify Long-Tail Keywords: Beyond basic terms, explore longer, more specific phrases that users search for. Examples include “coffee shops near me” or “green sneakers with glow in the dark laces.” These targeted keywords help you connect with a precise audience.
  5. Organize and Prioritize Keywords: Once you have a comprehensive list, categorize your keywords into relevant themes. Then, prioritize them based on their potential to help you achieve your defined goals.
  6. Monitor Performance and Refine Strategy: After implementing your chosen keywords, continuously track their performance. Be prepared to make adjustments. Some phrases might underperform, while others may prove more effective.

Don’t Forget: Staying informed about search trends and potential shifts in your target audience’s search behavior is crucial for maintaining a competitive online presence. Regularly reviewing and adapting your keyword strategy ensures your website remains visible.

]]> When potential customers visit your website, they quickly form an impression. Good UX/UI draws them in, while poor design can drive them away. Did you know that 94% of first impressions are related to design?

But what is UX and UI?

  • UX (User Experience): Focuses on how smooth or frustrating your site feels. 
  • UI (User Interface): Focuses on your site’s look and functionality. Attractive, easy-to-use sites are seen as more trustworthy.

Consider this:

  • Have you ever left a website because it was too complicated?
  • Do you keep returning to sites because they’re easy and enjoyable?

This blog explores how strategic UX/UI enhancements can solve common issues, improve engagement, and boost retention for businesses. Ready to transform your site? Let’s dive in.

1. Make First Impressions Count

  • Quick Judgments: It takes just 50 milliseconds for users to form an opinion about a website. That’s less than a blink of an eye!!
  • Professional & Relevant: Ensure your website conveys professionalism and trustworthiness immediately, or you risk losing potential customers.
  • Design Elements: Use clean, intuitive UI design with easy navigation, appealing layouts, and mobile responsiveness to make a strong first impression.

2. Simplify Navigation to Keep Users Engaged

  • Ease of Use: Your website should be as navigable as a physical store. If customers can’t find what they’re looking for, they’ll leave.
  • Intuitive Design: Employ clear menus and categories with a logical flow to enhance the user journey from visitor to customer.
  • Business Impact: A well-designed UX increases on-site time, reduces bounce rates, and boosts conversions.

3. Build Trust with Professional Design

  • Importance of Trust: Trust is crucial for customer retention, and your site’s appearance can significantly influence it.
  • Modern Design: Use modern, clean design elements and consistent branding to promote professionalism.
  • Enhance Credibility: Incorporate trust signals like customer testimonials, secure payment icons, and clear privacy policies.
