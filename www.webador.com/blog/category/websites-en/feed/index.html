]]> Creating your own website is a fun first step for your business. You choose a design, write your copy, add photos, and before you know it, your website is online! However, there are a number of pitfalls that starting entrepreneurs encounter. Don’t worry, because making mistakes is part of the process. But why make them when you can prevent them?

In this blog, we share the 10 most common mistakes and how to avoid them. This way, you get the most out of your website right from the start!

1. A domain name that is too long or complicated

Your domain name is your business’s online address and often one of the first choices you have to make. It is therefore important to think carefully about it. A good domain name is short, easy to remember, and simple to type. Avoid difficult spelling, long combinations, and unnecessary numbers or punctuation marks. The simpler, the better.

Ask yourself the question: can someone remember my domain name if I only mention it once?

2. Log in with a business email address

This is one of the most common pitfalls! Suppose you are a baker and have a business email address . It seems logical to use this email to log in to Webador as well. However, this can cause problems if your domain name or website is temporarily unavailable.

Your mailbox is linked to your domain name, so if your domain name is down, your mailbox won’t work either, and if you need to recover your password at that exact moment, you won’t be able to receive the recovery email!

Therefore, use a personal email address for your Webador account that you always have access to. You can then use your business email for communication with your customers.

3. You forget to publish your website

You’ve worked hard on your website and are completely satisfied with the end result. Time to send the link to your friends and family! But wait… they can’t see your website? How is this possible?!

Conclusion: your website is not yet published. Don’t forget to click the ‘publish’ button in the top right of the editor . Only then will your website be visible to others.

4. Too much information in the footer

The footer is the bottom of your website. Because it appears on every page of your website, it can be tempting to put all kinds of information in it, but an overcrowded footer actually makes your website look cluttered. Think carefully about what a visitor actually needs.

Therefore, use the footer for information that is relevant on multiple pages. Think of your contact details, social media channels, terms and conditions, and company details.

5. The receiving address of your contact form

When you add a contact form to your website, the system automatically uses the email address you are logged in with to Webador. Do you want customer inquiries to arrive in your business email? Then manually adjust the recipient address in the contact form settings.

TIP: Test your contact form before sharing your website. Fill out the form yourself and check if the email arrives correctly. This way, you avoid missing an important customer inquiry.

6. Not checking the mobile views

Your website might look great on a computer screen, but how does it look on your smartphone? About 52% view your website on mobile, so this is at least as important. JouwWeb uses responsive designs, so your website automatically adapts to the screen on which it is viewed. Nevertheless, it is smart to regularly check the mobile version yourself.

In the Editor, you can easily switch to the mobile view and view your website from a smartphone perspective:

7. An overcrowded menu

It might sound logical to give every page a place in your menu, but for your visitor, this can actually be confusing. The more choices you provide, the harder it becomes to find something quickly. Therefore, start with a simple structure, such as:

  • Home
  • About us
  • Services / Webshop
  • Contact

Do you need more information? Then you can always further expand the pages with subpages .

8. The expectation that you will immediately appear at the top of Google

You have published your website and, naturally, you immediately search for your company name on Google, but you aren’t appearing yet?!
Don’t panic. It is normal for a new website not to appear high in the search results immediately. Google needs to discover your website and index the pages first. Even if your website is indexed, it doesn’t automatically mean you will be at the top right away!

SEO is a process that requires time and attention. Do you want to improve your visibility? Then make sure you write clear copy and incorporate your keywords into it.

Do you have a Business package? Then we also recommend linking your website to Google Search Console to gain more insight into your visibility.

9. Multiple accounts

Have you lost your login details? It can be tempting to simply create a new account. However, that is not the most convenient solution. This can result in multiple accounts, meaning you won’t know which account belongs to which website later on. Therefore, always try to use one main account for your website.

Are you having trouble logging in? Then use the option to reset your password . Still having trouble? Then you can of course contact us via support@webador.com

10. You are unaware of our socials!

You might build a website yourself, but that doesn’t mean you have to figure everything out on your own. In our help center, you will find extensive explanations and step-by-step guides on a wide range of topics. From setting up your domain name and customizing your design, to your webshop and connecting external tools!

Do you prefer watching videos? Then check out our YouTube channel , where you will find practical instructional videos. Think of videos about SEO, webshops, and external tools.

Of course, we also share new tips, inspiration, and updates daily via Instagram and Facebook !

]]> When the world slowed down during the pandemic, many people found themselves turning back to forgotten hobbies and passions. For Leah O’Grady, a digital illustrator and maker from the West of Ireland, that quiet time became the spark for a flourishing creative business.

Leah launched Leah O’Grady Art in 2020, transforming her lifelong love of drawing into a brand that celebrates Ireland’s cultural heritage. Her work focuses on two beloved icons: nostalgic Irish pub fronts and vintage postal stamps. With over 60 pubs illustrated so far from the famous city institutions to the small, rural gems Leah is determined to give each one its moment to shine.

“Many of these pubs have sadly been closing after generations of serving their communities,” Leah shares. “Through my art, I hope to capture their spirit, evoke memories, and celebrate the role they’ve played in Irish life.”

From Hobby to Business

Creativity had always been part of Leah’s life. Alongside her work as a Special Needs Assistant in a primary school, she enjoyed arts and crafts both in the classroom and at home. But it wasn’t until 2020 that she began creating pieces more seriously.

At first, her illustrations were just for herself and her family. But when she shared them online, the response was overwhelming. People not only loved her nostalgic designs – they wanted to buy them. That encouragement was the turning point: her passion project became a small business.

Sharing a Piece of Ireland

The heart of Leah’s work lies in memory, connection, and identity. Her illustrations of cosy pub fronts and vintage stamps aren’t just decorative, they carry with them stories of belonging.

“Many young people have left Ireland to live abroad, and I think there’s a deep grá – a love and longing – for home that never really leaves them,”

Her designs, available as prints, coasters, cushions, tote bags, and more, have traveled far beyond Ireland. From England to America to Australia, they offer people a way to bring a little piece of Ireland into their daily lives.

Building an Online Presence

Like many creative entrepreneurs, Leah’s first step was sharing her art on social media and attending local craft markets. These markets remain an important part of her journey especially around Christmas when people are searching for meaningful gifts but their reach is naturally limited.

That’s why her website has been essential. Acting as both a portfolio and shopfront, it allows customers to explore her full range and purchase easily from anywhere in the world.

Often at markets, someone might love a piece but prefer to take a card and browse online later. My website makes that possible.”

It also streamlines the business side of things such as automating order confirmations and shipping notifications, while offering interactive features like polls where customers can suggest the next pub to be illustrated.

Overcoming Challenges

Like many artists, Leah has faced moments of self-doubt. Pricing her work was another early challenge, balancing the hours of design, production, and market days with customer expectations.

Her solution? Trusting her creative vision and connecting with other small business owners for support.

“Networking has been incredibly helpful,” she explains. “Talking to other like-minded artists allows you to share ideas, get advice, and gain confidence to keep going.”

Memorable Milestones

For Leah, success is measured not only in sales but in the stories her art creates. One standout moment was when a customer purchased two Galway Hooker cushions – which later ended up being presented to Irish President Michael D. Higgins and his wife Sabina at Áras an Uachtaráin.

Looking Ahead

Her mission is clear: to continue expanding her collection of Irish pubs and vintage stamps, celebrating the places and symbols that hold a special place in people’s hearts.

As Leah puts it:

]]> “People buy with their eyes.” It is a well-known saying in the e-commerce world. According to research by BigCommerce, 75% of online shoppers consider the quality of product photos to be the deciding factor in their purchase. In a physical store, a customer can touch a product, but in your webshop, your photos are the only ‘tangible’ experience.

Fortunately, with the right techniques and a good phone (or camera), you can get a long way. In this blog, we share 3 tips for taking good product photos.

Tip #1: Control the light

Light is the most important thing for a good photo. As an entrepreneur, natural daylight is the best (and cheapest) to use for your product photos.

  • Use a (large) window for daylight: You don’t need studio lights if you harness the power of natural light. You don’t have to wait until the sun is shining brightly, as this creates deep shadows. Instead, choose a slightly cloudy day, so that the light is distributed evenly over your product.
  • Troubled by shadows? Then take a piece of white cardboard or some aluminum foil and place it on the dark side. This ensures that the light is reflected and fills in the shadows.
  • Use a single light source: do not mix daylight with artificial light. This prevents color variations and ensures that all your product photos have the same professional look.

Tip #2: The Power of Composition

A cluttered background causes customers to lose interest quickly. You want to ensure that all attention goes to the product.

The infinity Curve

Do you want a super sleek background? Then create an infinity curve yourself . You do this by taking a large white sheet of paper and taping it to the top of your wall. Let the rest run across the table in a gentle curve. This causes the horizon line in your photo to disappear, making your background appear endless.

The Rule of Thirds

Don’t always place your product exactly in the center. Use the grid on your phone. According to
the Rule of Thirds , a photo looks more interesting if the subject is positioned at one of the intersections of the lines.

Make clear how big your product is

A common reason for returns is that a product is often larger or smaller than expected. Therefore, always include a photo of the product in use. Photograph it next to an everyday object so the customer immediately sees its size.

3. Post-processing and Optimization

A good product photo is only truly complete after the finishing touch, but pay attention, because post-processing is different from using filters! It is important that you adjust/check the following things:

  1. The brightness of your photo: focus on increasing the exposure and contrast. Do not use filters, only these two functions!
  2. File size vs. speed: the speed at which your images load is crucial. Large images (5+ MB) slow down your website. Use tools like TinyPNG to reduce the size of your photos without loss of quality.
  3. SEO optimization: As soon as you upload your photos to JouwWeb, give them a name, for example handmade-leather-jacket-brown.png

Get started!

Product photography is easier than you think! Take your time, experiment with different angles and spaces, and take a close look at what your competitors are doing. With these 3 tips, you can at least lay a good foundation for a webshop that looks great!

]]> Summer is just around the corner, and you’re probably already looking forward to that well-deserved vacation! But as an entrepreneur, there’s often a little voice in your head: ” What actually happens to my website/webshop while I’m relaxing on the beach? “

Research into consumer behavior shows that customers are quite willing to wait, provided the communication is clear beforehand. After all, transparency builds trust!

With this holiday checklist, you ensure that everything is well organized before you leave.

1. Manage your customers’ expectations

Ignorance is the biggest conversion killer! Therefore, you need to be transparent with your website visitors:

  • The Top Bar : In the Webador editor, you can easily place a top bar at the top of your website.
    A short message on your website can prevent many questions. Let visitors know that you are on vacation and what this means for orders or inquiries.
  • Update your contact page: Mention on this page that response times are longer than usual. Optionally, you can create an FAQ page and direct visitors there so they can find the most frequently asked questions themselves!

2. Enable an automatic email response

An automatic out-of-office message is a simple way to manage expectations. As soon as someone sends you an email, they immediately receive a reply stating that you are on vacation and when you can respond again.

This way, customers feel heard, even if you cannot answer immediately.

Here is how to set up an out-of-office message for:
  1. Your Webmail
  2. Outlook
  3. Gmail

3. Check your webshop stock

Do you have a webshop? Then there are two options possible as soon as you go on vacation:

  • Option A : You keep your webshop open but communicate everywhere that delivery times are longer than usual. Don’t forget to mention when you will be back and when you can ship the order!
  • Option B : You temporarily disable the shopping cart in the settings. Customers will then not be able to order anything from your webshop, as it will be (temporarily) offline.

Even when you are on vacation, your online visibility doesn’t have to come to a complete standstill. By scheduling some content for your socials in advance, your company remains visible without you having to work on it daily.   • Canva
  • Later

]]> When building a website or online store, you might primarily think about images, colors, and products. Yet, something you don’t immediately see plays an equally important role: the fonts you use. A good font combination ensures that your website looks professional, is easy to read, and has a beautiful appearance. Crucial to your online success!

In this blog post, we’ll guide you step-by-step through choosing and combining fonts. We’ll provide practical tips and examples that you can easily apply yourself. This way, you can get started right away and ensure your texts look their best.

1. Types of Fonts

Before you start combining fonts, it’s helpful to know what types of fonts there are, and what each type is used for.

  • Serif: These are fonts with small “feet” or strokes at the end of the letters. They are often used in classic, elegant, and formal settings. This font is good for titles, but less ideal for longer texts.
  • Sans-serif: A typeface that is often uncluttered and easier to read on a screen. This is ideal for longer texts on websites.
  • Decorative fonts: Think of display fonts, handwritten styles, ornate lettering, etc. These are great for visual elements or as headings on your website. Just make sure they remain legible.

Tip: Keep it simple. Choose one primary font for all your text and consider one eye-catching font for your logo and headlines.

2. The importance of font combinations

You might be wondering if using multiple fonts is actually necessary. The answer is: not necessarily, but it can give your website a lot more power.

A good font combination helps you with:

  1. Recognition and atmosphere: say you sell handmade jewelry — then a sleek display font for your headlines works well, combined with a clean sans-serif for your product descriptions.
  2. Readability and structure: different styles between headings and your general text make it easier for your visitor to scan your page
  3. Professionalism: An incorrectly chosen font can make visitors doubt the quality of your website.

3. What should you pay attention to when combining fonts?

There are 4 things to keep in mind when combining your fonts:

  1. Character & Appearance:
    Ask yourself: what kind of feeling do I want to evoke? Playful, reliable, luxurious, warm? Make sure your fonts reflect that.
  2. Structure & Hierarchy:
    Ensure a clear distinction in size or style between your headings and body text. This helps your visitors quickly see what’s important.
  3. Contrast & Harmony:
    A good combination should have enough difference to differentiate, but also enough similarities to be cohesive.
  4. Stuck? Keep it simple!
    At a loss? Then choose a single font with multiple styles (light, regular, Italian, bold). This creates a sense of calm and prevents mismatches.
    For example: use “Poppins Regular” for your body text and “Poppins Bold” for your headings.

4. Common mistakes

Be aware of these pitfalls when combining fonts:

  • Too many fonts at once: Do you have three or more different fonts on a page? That can quickly become chaotic! Stick to a maximum of two fonts.
  • Decorative font as body text : No matter how beautiful a decorative font is, if your font is difficult to read, visitors will quickly drop out.
  • No attention to mobile: Always test whether your fonts are also easy to read on the mobile version of your website.
  • Lack of consistency: Use the same font combination on every page. This creates cohesion and recognizability.

A well-chosen font combination may be subtle, but it can have a big impact. It increases readability, creates recognition, and creates a professional look.

What’s going on?

Scammers send emails that look very real, with logos and layouts resembling those of Webador. The contents of these messages are often misleading. They frequently ask for:

  • Login credentials: They claim your account is blocked or request that you log in again to prevent that.
  • Invoice payments: You receive an urgent notice of an outstanding invoice or an expired domain. Typically, you’re asked to pay immediately via an external link.
  • Data verification: They ask you to “verify” your personal or business information via a form.

Your defense: 4 simple habits

Make the following steps a habit. They cost only a few extra seconds, but can save you a lot of trouble.

  1. Check the sender’s address. The sender’s name tells you nothing. Always click or tap the name to see the full email address. We always send emails from an address ending in “@webador.com.”
  2. Inspect a link before clicking. Hover your mouse over the link. In the lower-left corner of your screen, you’ll see the real web address. Does it look strange? Don’t click. Links in our emails always point to an address that starts with www.webador.com, or help.webador.com / www.jouwweb.nl or help.jouwweb.nl.
  3. Payments always go through the Dashboard. Your invoices and payment options are only available in your Webador dashboard. We will never ask you via email to make a payment through an external link.
  4. Phone calls. We will never ask you to call us about a security problem or payment issue.

What to do if you receive a fake email?

If you get an email you don’t trust, follow these steps:

  1. DO NOTHING. Don’t click anything, don’t download attachments, and don’t reply.
  2. Delete the email.
  3. When in doubt, log in manually to your Webador dashboard. There you can see the current status of your account.
  4. Report the issue. Contact us via our help center or via support@webador.com to report suspicious emails. We’re happy to look into it.

  • Choose a strong password. Always use a unique password for your Webador account and change it regularly. In this article, you can read how to update your password.
  • Enable two-step verification (MFA). This adds an extra security layer. Even if someone guesses your password, they can’t get in without the code from your phone. Use it for your Webador account, as well as for your social media and other important accounts.
  • Keep your software updated. Keep the software on your computer, phone, and browser up to date. Updates often include the latest security patches.
